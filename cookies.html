Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how Ashford & Vale Recovery Counsel uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit our website. It explains what these technologies are, why we use them, and the choices available to you.

Last updated: July 27, 2026 · Effective immediately

01 What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your computer, smartphone, or other device when you visit a website. They allow websites to recognize your device, remember preferences, improve functionality, and provide information about how visitors interact with the site.

Cookies generally do not give us direct access to your computer or personal files. Instead, they help us deliver a faster, safer, and more personalized experience.

02 Why We Use Cookies

Ashford & Vale uses cookies to improve the performance, security, and usability of our website.

Cookies help us:

  • Keep the website operating properly.
  • Remember your preferences where applicable.
  • Improve website performance and reliability.
  • Understand how visitors use our website.
  • Protect against fraudulent or malicious activity.

We do not use cookies to sell your personal information.

03 Types of Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly. Without them, certain features and security protections may not operate.

Analytics Cookies

These cookies collect anonymous information about how visitors use our website, such as pages viewed, navigation patterns, and website performance. This information helps us improve the user experience.

Preference Cookies

Preference cookies remember settings you choose, such as language or other display preferences, making future visits more convenient.

04 Third-Party Cookies

Some features on our website may rely on trusted third-party providers. These providers may place cookies through our website to support analytics, embedded content, payment processing, or other services.

Examples may include website analytics providers, payment processors, cloud-hosting services, and embedded communication tools.

We do not control third-party cookies. Their use is governed by the privacy policies of the organizations providing those services.

05 Managing Cookies

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings.

You can usually:

  • View stored cookies.
  • Delete existing cookies.
  • Block future cookies.
  • Allow cookies only from selected websites.
  • Receive notifications before cookies are stored.
Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality and prevent some features from operating as intended.

06 Cookie Retention

Different cookies remain on your device for different periods of time.

  • Session Cookies remain only while your browser is open and are automatically deleted when you close it.
  • Persistent Cookies remain on your device for a specified period or until you delete them manually through your browser settings.

The length of time a cookie is stored depends on its purpose and the configuration established by the service providing it.

07 Do Not Track Signals

Some web browsers offer a "Do Not Track" (DNT) feature that allows users to indicate a preference regarding online tracking.

Because there is currently no universally accepted standard governing how websites should respond to DNT signals, our website does not automatically respond differently when such signals are received.

You may still manage cookies at any time through your browser settings.

08 Changes to This Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our website, technology, legal requirements, or business practices.

When changes are made, we will revise the "Last Updated" date shown at the top of this page. Material changes may also be communicated through notices on our website where appropriate.

Your continued use of the website after any updates constitutes acceptance of the revised Cookie Policy.

If you have questions about this Cookie Policy or how Ashford & Vale Recovery Counsel uses cookies and similar technologies, please contact us through our Contact page or by using the phone number or email address listed there.
