About Us

At Ashford & Vale, we believe that money owed to individuals and businesses shouldn't remain undiscovered or unclaimed. Our mission is to help clients identify potential recovery opportunities and guide them through the process with professionalism, transparency, and care.

We are a family-owned business founded by university graduates who combined their academic backgrounds, research skills, and passion for solving complex problems to create a service focused on helping others. What began as an idea has grown into a business dedicated to making financial recovery more accessible and easier to navigate.

Using specialized research methods, trusted data sources, and a structured verification process, we assess potential recovery opportunities and provide clients with clear, practical guidance. Whether the matter involves unclaimed funds or other eligible financial recoveries, we approach every case with diligence and confidentiality.

01Our Approach

We understand that every situation is unique. That's why we begin with a careful assessment of each case before recommending the next steps. Our process is designed to be transparent from the start, so clients understand what we do, what our services include, and how our fees are structured.

We are committed to:

  • Integrity — Acting honestly and ethically in every interaction.
  • Transparency — Providing clear information about our services, fees, and process.
  • Confidentiality — Treating your personal and financial information with care and respect.
  • Professionalism — Delivering reliable, research-driven services with attention to detail.
  • Client Focus — Keeping your interests at the center of everything we do.

02Why Choose Us?

We know that trust is earned. That's why we focus on clear communication, thorough research, and a professional experience from your first inquiry to the completion of your case. Our goal is not simply to identify potential recovery opportunities, but to provide knowledgeable guidance throughout the process.

When you work with Ashford & Vale, you're choosing a team that values accuracy, discretion, and long-term relationships. We take pride in helping clients understand their options and move forward with confidence.

03Our Mission

To provide trusted, research-driven financial recovery services that help individuals and businesses identify and pursue funds they may be entitled to receive, while maintaining the highest standards of integrity, transparency, and professionalism.

04Our Vision

To become a recognized leader in financial recovery services by delivering exceptional client experiences, fostering trust, and setting a standard for ethical, transparent, and research-based solutions.
